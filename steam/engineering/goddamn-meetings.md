# Goddamn Meetings

Effective meeting agendas are structured around topics, with each topic typically lasting two to three minutes and grouped into sections of five to ten. The agenda's flow should resemble a bell curve: start with quick, easy items, place the more difficult or complex topics in the middle, and then "sandwich" it by ending with faster items. However, the most crucial principle is to ensure a logical progression of topics. The agenda should always conclude with a section for "other" business.
